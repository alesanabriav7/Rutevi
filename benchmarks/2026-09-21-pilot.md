# Pilot: 2026-09-21

Nine real Codex executions; one attempt per task and strategy.
[Raw evidence](2026-09-21-pilot.json) · [Methodology](README.md).

All strategies passed 3/3 tasks. Jev selected Luna low / medium / medium,
avoiding all three frontier executions without losing checked task success.
However, Jev was **14.4% slower than fixed Astra** and **8.3% slower than fixed Luna**
in this single run. Astra's ledger execution was substantially faster.
These observations do not establish a statistically reliable latency difference.

Routing took 555–692 ms per decision (1.839 s total). Jev itself consumed
10,109 input and 1,092 output tokens, in addition to the worker usage below.
The extra wall time cannot be attributed solely to routing; executor behavior and
cache state differed. No monetary savings were measured.

**Supported finding:** these three tasks did not require a frontier executor to pass.
**Not established:** better model selection than always using Luna, billed savings,
or cases where Terra/Astra improve success. Do not market this pilot as proof of those claims.
The suite and grading checks were fixed before the run; no failed results were removed
and no executor was retried.
